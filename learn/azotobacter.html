Azotobacter

Azotobacter is a free-living nitrogen-fixing bacterium found in soil. It fixes atmospheric nitrogen in the soil, making it available to plants, especially non-leguminous crops (unlike Rhizobium which requires legumes).

Nitrogen fixation Fixes ~20–40 kg/ha/year of atmospheric nitrogen

Growth hormone production Produces auxins, gibberellins, and cytokinins for better plant growth

Biocontrol Suppresses soil-borne pathogens like Fusarium, Pythium, Rhizoctonia

Improves soil fertility Enhances microbial diversity and soil health

Stress tolerance Helps plants tolerate drought and salinity

Cost-effective Reduces need for chemical nitrogen fertilizers

Seed Treatment :

Mix 200 g Azotobacter culture per 10 kg of seed with a sticker (e.g., 10% jaggery or rice gruel). Dry in shade and sow immediately.

Soil Application :

Mix 2–4 kg Azotobacter (powder) or 2–4 L (liquid) with 100–200 kg compost/FYM and broadcast per acre before sowing.

Drip Irrigation or Drenching :

Dilute 1–2 L Azotobacter liquid in 200 L chlorine-free water per acre.

Item Quantity

Azotobacter Starter Culture 1–2 L (liquid) or 1 kg (solid)

Jaggery or molasses 1.5 – 2 kg

Chickpea flour or rice flour 500 g

Clean chlorine-free water 200 L

Cow dung or compost slurry (optional) 2–3 kg

Aeration pump or stirring setup Optional but recommended

Plastic drum (food-grade) 200 L capacity

Muslin cloth or lid For covering

Prepare base solution :

Boil 2 kg jaggery in 5 L water. Cool and mix with 200 L clean water.

Add 500 g chickpea flour or rice flour as carbon/nutrient source.

Inoculate :

Add 1–2 L Azotobacter liquid starter to the solution.

Optionally add 2–3 kg cow dung or compost slurry for microbial diversity.

Fermentation :

Keep the drum in a shaded, ventilated area.

Stir daily or use an aeration pump for 15–30 minutes/day.

Cover loosely with muslin cloth to avoid contamination.

Duration :

Allow to ferment for 5–7 days .

Check for froth, mild sour smell, and no foul odor.

Ready-to-use :

Use immediately or store in a cool, shaded area for 2–5 days.

Soil application Mix 20–25 L with 100 kg compost/FYM

Drip irrigation 10–15 L in 200 L water

Seedling dip Dip roots in diluted solution (1:10) for 30 min

Foliar spray Not recommended for Azotobacter

Water : Use only chlorine-free water.

Fungicide : Do not apply fungicide 5–7 days before or after Azotobacter use.

Storage : Store in a cool, dark place, away from sunlight.

Crop Suitability : Suitable for rice, wheat, maize, sugarcane, cotton, vegetables, banana, turmeric, ginger , and many fruit trees .
