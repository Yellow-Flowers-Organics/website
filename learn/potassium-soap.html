Potassium soap

Potassium Soap is a natural insecticidal soap made from potassium hydroxide (KOH) and plant-based oils (like coconut, castor, neem, or sunflower oil) . It’s used for controlling soft-bodied pests such as aphids, whiteflies, mites, thrips, and mealybugs by disrupting their cell membranes and suffocating them .

Type: Natural contact insecticide (soap-based)

Active Agent: Potassium salts of fatty acids

Mode of Action: Disrupts insect cuticle membranes and respiration

Feature Benefits

Contact-based pesticide Kills soft-bodied insects without harming beneficial predators

Organic & residue-free Safe for humans, pollinators, and environment

Easy to prepare on-farm Low-cost and scalable for small to large farms

Washes off with water No phytotoxicity when used in correct dilution

Dual action Insect control + mild antifungal effect on leaf surface

Compatible with neem oil & botanicals Can be mixed with neem oil for enhanced effect

Application Type Dosage & Method

Foliar spray (standalone) 1.5% to 2% solution (15–20 mL per liter of water)

Foliar spray (with neem oil) 15 mL potassium soap + 5 mL neem oil per liter + 1 mL emulsifier (soap)

Frequency Every 5–7 days or after pest reappearance

Time Early morning or late afternoon

Saponification Reaction :

Oil (fatty acid) + Potassium Hydroxide (KOH) → Potassium Soap + Glycerin

Ingredient Quantity

Plant-based oil (e.g., castor, coconut, sunflower) 2 liters

Potassium hydroxide (KOH) flakes/pellets 250 grams

Clean chlorine-free water 7–8 liters (approx.)

Steel/glass container 1

Wooden/plastic stirrer 1

Gloves & goggles For safety (KOH is caustic)

⚠️ Safety Note : Always add KOH to water , never water to KOH. Work in a well-ventilated space.

Dissolve KOH :

Slowly add 250 g KOH into 1 liter of water while stirring until fully dissolved.

Heat the oil (optional) :

Slightly warm 2 liters of oil (not above 60°C) to speed up saponification.

Mix oil and KOH solution :

Slowly pour KOH solution into the oil while stirring continuously. Stir for 30–60 minutes .

Rest :

Let the mixture rest for 12–24 hours . It will thicken and become a smooth, soap-like liquid.

Dilute for storage :

Add remaining water (up to 10 L total volume) and mix well. This diluted soap concentrate can be used for spray.

Dilution for Use Rate

Regular foliar use 15–20 mL/L water

With neem oil 15 mL soap + 5 mL neem oil + 1 mL emulsifier per liter water

With garlic/chili extracts Combine with decoctions for broader pest coverage

✅ Do's ❌ Don'ts

Wear gloves during preparation Don’t inhale KOH vapors

Use on cloudy days or evening Avoid hot midday sprays

Store soap concentrate in airtight containers Don’t expose to moisture or air for long

Test on few leaves first Don’t spray during flowering if unsure
