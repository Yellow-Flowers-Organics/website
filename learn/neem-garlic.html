Neem and garlic

Neem/Garlic Bio-Insecticide

Here's a complete guide on Neem/Garlic-Based Bio-Insecticide, including key features, benefits, usage, multiplication, and application details:

Organic & Eco-Friendly : Made from natural ingredients like neem and garlic.

Broad-Spectrum Action: Effective against a wide range of sucking and chewing pests (aphids, whiteflies, thrips, mealybugs, caterpillars, mites, etc.).

Antifeedant & Repellent: Discourages pests from feeding and laying eggs.

Fungal & Bacterial Control: Provides mild control over some fungal infections.

Safe for Beneficial Insects: Less harmful to bees, butterflies, and predators if applied during non-peak hours.

Cost-effective alternative to chemical pesticides.

Reduces pest resistance build-up over time.

Can be used in organic-certified farms.

Easy to prepare and use at the farm level.

Enhances plant health and immunity.

This creates a concentrated microbial extract for bulk use.

📦 Materials Needed

Neem leaves or kernels: 5 kg

Garlic cloves (crushed): 500 g – 1 kg

Cow urine (fermented): 10 liters

Jaggery or molasses: 1 kg

Water: 40 liters

Plastic drum or container (capacity 50–60 liters)

Stick for stirring, muslin cloth for covering

🧑‍🔬 Procedure

Crush neem leaves/kernels and garlic into a paste.

Mix in cow urine and water in the container.

Add jaggery to promote microbial activity.

Stir well once or twice daily using a wooden stick.

Cover with muslin cloth (not airtight) to allow airflow.

Let it ferment for 10–15 days in the shade.

🧪 Final Concentrate:

Strain the liquid and store it in a cool place.

Shelf life: up to 30 days if kept away from sunlight and heat.

For 100 liters of water:

Neem Oil (preferably 85% or higher): 300–500 ml

Garlic Paste (optional if already in formulation): 500 g (if fresh garlic is used)

Emulsifier (like surf or liquid soap): 50–100 ml

Stir well until oil and water form a uniform emulsion.

Spray early morning or late evening to avoid leaf burn and protect pollinators.

For Foliar Spray:

Use 5–10 liters of this fermented solution per 100 liters of water.

Spray on both sides of leaves and stems.

Frequency:

Preventive: Once every 10–15 days.

Curative: Every 7 days for 2–3 sprays in case of infestation.

Suitable Crops:

All vegetable crops, citrus, guava, mango, pomegranate, pulses, cereals, and spices.

Best Time to Spray : Early morning or after 4:30 PM

Do Not Mix with : Copper-based fungicides or strong chemical pesticides.

Test First : Always do a patch test on a few plants before spraying large areas.

Use Clean Water : Avoid hard or muddy water for better results.

Store Away from Sunlight : Keep the concentrate in a shaded, cool place.
