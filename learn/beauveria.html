Beauveria bassiana

Beauveria bassiana is a fungal entomopathogen that infects a wide range of insect pests, including whiteflies, aphids, thrips, mealybugs, jassids, and borers . It works by attaching to the insect cuticle, germinating, and growing into the insect body, eventually killing it.

Broad pest control range Works on 200+ insect species

Environment-friendly No toxic residues

Systemic through cuticle Penetrates insect body, kills from inside

Compatible with IPM systems Can be used with Neem oil, Bt, and waste decomposer

Application Mode Dosage per 15 L Remarks

Foliar Spray 10–15 g/mL Spray on underside of leaves in evening

Soil Drenching 20 g or mL in 10 L Controls soil-borne pests like grubs

Root Dip 1–2 g/mL per L Dip seedlings before transplanting

Item Quantity

Beauveria starter culture 100–200 mL or 20–50 g

Jaggery or glucose 200 g

Gram flour or rice flour 200 g

Chlorine-free Water 10 L

Plastic drum/bucket 10–15 L

Muslin cloth or lid Cover

Aeration (stir or pump) Optional

Prepare Nutrient Broth:

Dissolve 200 g jaggery in warm water, add to 10 L clean water.

Add 200 g gram flour or rice flour, stir to mix.

Inoculate Beauveria Culture:

Add 100–200 mL liquid or 50 g powder culture.

Ferment:

Cover loosely and place in shade.

Stir twice daily or aerate gently.

Ready in 5 – 7 Days :

Should have a musty odor and froth.

Foliar Spray : Mix 10–15 L multiplied Beauveria in 200 L water.

Soil drenching : Mix 10–20 L with compost slurry and apply at root zone.

Use in evening or cloudy conditions for fungal survival.

Do’s Don’ts

Use chlorine-free water Don’t mix with fungicides

Apply in moist, shaded hours (evening) Avoid in strong sun or rain

Store in cool shade for max 3 days Don’t keep under direct sunlight

Can be combined with Neem/Waste Decomposer Don’t apply during pesticide sprays
