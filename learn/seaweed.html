Seaweed extract

Seaweed extract is a bio-stimulant derived from marine algae (commonly Ascophyllum nodosum , Sargassum , Laminaria , etc.). It is rich in natural plant hormones (auxins, cytokinins, gibberellins), trace elements , amino acids , vitamins , and polysaccharides that promote healthy plant growth and stress resistance.

Feature Benefit

Rich in plant growth hormones Promotes cell elongation, shoot/root growth

Improves flowering & fruiting Enhances yield and quality of produce

Boosts stress resistance Helps plants cope with drought, salinity, and temperature fluctuations

Enhances root development Stimulates deeper and fibrous rooting

Improves microbial activity Enhances soil fertility and microbial diversity

Increases nutrient uptake Improves absorption of macro & micronutrients

Organic and eco-friendly Safe for environment, soil, and beneficial insects

Application Method Dosage Frequency

Foliar Spray 2–5 mL/liter of water Every 10–15 days

Drip Irrigation 1–2 liters/acre of diluted solution Monthly or crop-specific stages

Seed Treatment 5 mL/liter (soak seeds for 30–60 minutes) Before sowing

Root Drenching 500 mL in 100 L water per acre At transplantation or stress

You can either use:

Commercial seaweed extract powder (available as flakes or granules)

Raw dried seaweed (less common unless you are near coastal areas)

Material Quantity

Seaweed extract powder or flakes 500 g – 1 kg

Chlorine-free water 100 liters

Jaggery or molasses (optional) 1 kg (for microbial boost)

Bio-decomposer (like OWDC/NWDC) 1 liter (optional)

Plastic drum (100–200 L capacity) 1

Stirring rod or submersible pump 1

Muslin cloth (for filtration) 1

Fill Drum : Add 80–90 liters of clean, chlorine-free water.

Add Seaweed Extract : Mix in 500 g to 1 kg of seaweed extract powder or flakes.

(Optional) Add Jaggery : Add 1 kg to support microbial activity.

(Optional) Add Bio-decomposer : To activate and multiply microbes, add 1 liter OWDC/NWDC.

Top-Up Water : Fill up to 100 liters.

Mix Well : Stir 2–3 times daily using a rod or submersible stirrer.

Ferment : Keep loosely covered for 24–48 hours in shade.

Filter : Use muslin cloth before using in sprayers or fertigation.

Use Type Dilution Ratio

Foliar Spray 2–5 mL per liter of water

Drip Irrigation 1–2 liters per acre

Root Drench 500 mL in 100 L of water

Seed Soaking 5 mL/L of water (soak for 30–60 mins)

✅ Highly beneficial for all crops , including:

Vegetables : Tomato, chili, brinjal, okra, spinach, cabbage

Fruits : Citrus, banana, guava, mango, papaya

Flowers : Marigold, rose, chrysanthemum

Cereals & Pulses : Wheat, rice, maize, soybean, moong

Plantation : Sugarcane, tea, coffee

Spices : Turmeric, ginger, cardamom, coriander

✅ Do's ❌ Don’ts

Use clean, chlorine-free water Do not mix with strong chemical pesticides

Stir daily during fermentation Avoid application during peak sunlight

Store away from heat & light Don’t exceed recommended doses

Filter before spraying Don’t store in metal containers

Condition Duration

Cool, shaded area 30–45 days

Airtight plastic container Prevents spoilage
