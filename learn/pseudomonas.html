Pseudomonas fluorescens

Pseudomonas fluorescens is a beneficial rhizobacterium known for promoting plant growth and controlling fungal pathogens. It colonizes the rhizosphere and competes with or inhibits disease-causing microbes through antibiotic production and induced systemic resistance.

✅ Controls fungal diseases like root rot, wilt, damping off, sheath blight, and leaf spots.

✅ Promotes plant growth by producing plant hormones (auxins, gibberellins).

✅ Induces systemic resistance (ISR) in plants against pathogens.

✅ Enhances nutrient availability (especially phosphorus).

✅ Compatible with most organic farming systems.

Seed Treatment 10 g per kg of seed. Soak in jaggery solution or sticker, dry in shade before sowing.

Root Dipping Mix 1–2 kg in 20–30 L water, dip seedlings for 30 minutes before transplant.

Soil Application Mix 2–5 kg/acre in 100 kg compost or FYM and broadcast at sowing or near roots.

Drip Irrigation 2–3 L (liquid) or 3–5 kg (powder) per acre with sufficient water.

Foliar Spray 5–10 ml per liter of water. Spray at 15–20 day intervals during early crop stages.

Pseudomonas fluorescens mother culture (liquid): 1 Liter

Jaggery or Molasses: 1 kg

Chickpea flour or Soya flour: 500 g

Cow dung slurry or FYM extract (optional): 2–3 liters

Chlorine-free water: 200 liters

Aeration device (submersible pump or aquarium air pump)

Clean plastic drum or tank (200L capacity)

Muslin cloth for covering

Prepare Base: Dissolve jaggery in 10–20 liters of warm water. Let it cool.

Mix Ingredients: Add chickpea/soy flour and cow dung slurry to the jaggery water and mix well.

Fill Drum: Pour this mixture into the clean 200L drum and top up with chlorine-free water.

Inoculate Culture: Add 1 liter of mother culture of Pseudomonas fluorescens .

Aerate the Mix: Use a submersible pump or aquarium aerator for 5–6 hours/day.

Fermentation: Keep the drum loosely covered with a cloth. Stir twice daily.

Ready in 5–7 days: A foamy surface and fermented smell indicate it's ready.

Always use chlorine-free water.

Avoid direct sunlight and rain—keep under shade.

Use within 7–10 days of preparation.

Clean the container before each batch.

Store in a cool, dark place if not used immediately.

For liquid: Use within 7–10 days.

For dry: Store in airtight packets in a cool, dry place for up to 3–6 months.

Compatible with Trichoderma , Bacillus subtilis , Beauveria bassiana (with staggered application).

Avoid mixing with copper-based fungicides or strong chemical pesticides.
