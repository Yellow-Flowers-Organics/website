Azospirillum

Azospirillum is a free-living, nitrogen-fixing bacteria that colonizes the root zones (rhizosphere and rhizoplane) of plants, especially grasses and cereals . It is known for associative symbiosis , improving plant nitrogen uptake, root growth, and resistance to abiotic stress.

Scientific Name: Azospirillum brasilense , A. lipoferum

Type: Plant Growth Promoting Rhizobacteria (PGPR)

Function: Biological Nitrogen Fixation (BNF)

Feature Benefit

Fixes atmospheric nitrogen Supplies 20–40 kg N/ha biologically

Promotes root growth Releases plant hormones (auxins, gibberellins, cytokinins)

Improves nutrient uptake Enhances phosphorus and micronutrient absorption

Drought resistance Improves water-use efficiency

Compatible with cereals, millets, grasses Particularly effective in monocots

Enhances seed germination Improves seedling vigor and crop stand

Application Method Dosage

Seed treatment 25–30 mL/kg seed (liquid) or 25–40 g/kg (powder)

Seedling root dip 1 L in 10 L water; dip roots for 20–30 minutes

Soil application 2–4 L/acre mixed with 50 kg FYM/compost

Drip irrigation 2–3 L/acre in 100–200 L water

Foliar spray (optional) 250 mL in 15 L water, early-stage only

Target Volume : 200 Liters

Duration : 5–7 days fermentation

Item Quantity

Azospirillum liquid culture (mother) 1 liter

Jaggery or molasses 2 kg

Besan (chickpea flour) – optional 250–500 g

Chlorine-free water 200 liters

Food-grade plastic drum 200-liter capacity

Wooden stick For stirring

Muslin cloth To loosely cover the drum

Dissolve 2 kg jaggery in 5–10 liters warm water.

Add 1 liter Azospirillum culture (liquid formulation).

(Optional) Add 250–500 g besan to boost microbial activity.

Fill drum with chlorine-free water to make 200 liters .

Stir well and cover with muslin cloth (do not seal).

Stir twice daily (morning and evening).

Keep drum in shaded location (avoid direct sunlight).

Fermentation completes in 5–7 days when smell becomes slightly sour-earthy and foam appears.

Method Rate & Dilution Frequency

Soil drenching 20–25 L/acre mixed with irrigation or FYM Every 30 days

Drip irrigation 10–15 L/acre through drip lines Monthly or as needed

Compost inoculation 5 L/ton of compost Once during composting

Seed/seedling treatment 1:10 dilution; soak seeds or dip roots for 30 mins Before sowing

✅ Do's ❌ Don'ts

Use clean, chlorine-free water Avoid chlorinated water or soap-contaminated drums

Always stir during fermentation Don’t leave undisturbed for long periods

Filter before use in drip/fogger Don’t clog emitters with solids

Apply in cooler part of day Avoid high-heat midday applications

Use solution within 30 days Don't store beyond a month after multiplication

Rice, wheat, maize, sorghum, pearl millet

Sugarcane, banana, turmeric, ginger

Grasses, fodder crops

Cotton, tea, coffee

Most monocots and fibrous-root plants

Legumes (use Rhizobium instead)

Tap-rooted vegetables (unless used in soil)

Form Shelf Life Storage Condition

Azospirillum Liquid (stock) 6 months Cool, dry, shaded environment

Multiplied solution 25–30 days Stir daily, store in shade
