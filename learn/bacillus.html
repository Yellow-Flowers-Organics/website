Bacillus

Bacillus is a genus of Gram-positive, rod-shaped bacteria that are commonly found in soil, water, and air. Many Bacillus species form endospores , which are highly resistant to heat, radiation, and chemicals—allowing them to survive harsh environmental conditions for extended periods.

Gram-positive

Aerobic or facultatively anaerobic

Rod-shaped

Spore-forming (most species)

Widely found in soil, compost, decaying vegetation, and the rhizosphere

Many are non-pathogenic and beneficial to plants

Bacillus subtilis Antifungal, plant growth promoter, induces systemic resistance (ISR)

Bacillus thuringiensis (Bt) Bio-insecticide; produces crystal proteins toxic to caterpillars, borers

Bacillus amyloliquefaciens Antifungal, produces antibiotics, enhances root growth

Bacillus megaterium Phosphate solubilizer, promotes plant growth

Bacillus pumilus Suppresses soil-borne pathogens, improves nutrient uptake

Bacillus licheniformis Produces enzymes (protease, cellulase), biocontrol agent

Bacillus cereus (non-pathogenic strains) Entomopathogenic, some biocontrol activity

Bacillus polymyxa (Paenibacillus polymyxa) Nitrogen fixer, biocontrol agent, produces antibiotics

Bacillus is not a single microbe but a diverse genus of bacteria with species playing critical roles in biocontrol, nutrient cycling, and plant growth promotion. Their resilience, ease of multiplication, and wide-spectrum benefits make them a vital part of biofertilizer and biopesticide formulations in organic and sustainable agriculture .

Prepare Nutrient Broth:

Dissolve 200 g jaggery in hot water and mix with 10 L clean water.

Add 200 g flour and stir well.

Inoculate Bt Culture:

Add 100–200 mL of Bt liquid or powder.

Ferment:

Keep the bucket in shade.

Stir twice daily or use an aeration pump.

Cover loosely with muslin cloth.

Ready in 3–5 Days :

Look for frothing and mild odor.

Use immediately or within 2–3 days.

Foliar Spray : Dilute 10–15 L multiplied Bt in 150–200 L water.

Use in early morning or evening for best effect.

Repeat every 7–10 days , especially in heavy pest infestation.
