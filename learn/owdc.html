OWDC

Organic Waste Decomposer (OWDC) is a microbial consortium developed by National Centre of Organic Farming (NCOF), India . It contains seven strains of beneficial bacteria (e.g., Lactobacillus, Bacillus , etc.) that decompose organic waste, convert it into compost rapidly, and improve soil microbial diversity.

Organic Waste Decomposer (OWDC) is a microbial consortium developed by National Centre of Organic Farming (NCOF), India . It contains seven strains of beneficial bacteria (e.g., Lactobacillus, Bacillus , etc.) that decompose organic waste, convert it into compost rapidly, and improve soil microbial diversity.

Multi-use Input

Converts organic waste to compost in 30–40 days

Improves beneficial microbial population

Decreases dependency on external compost and fertilizers

Helps degrade pesticide and herbicide residues in soil

Suppresses pathogens through competitive colonization

Composting, foliar spray, soil drenching, seed treatment, etc.

Composting Add OWDC solution over compost pile every 5–7 days

Soil Drench Apply 10–20 L OWDC solution per acre near root zone or via drip

Foliar Spray Mix 200 mL OWDC in 15 L water; spray every 15 days

Seed Treatment Soak seeds for 10–30 minutes in diluted OWDC (1:10)

Residue Decomposition Spray over crop residues and plough in for rapid degradation
