Acetobacter

Acetobacter is a nitrogen-fixing bacteria that lives inside plant tissues (endophytic) of crops like sugarcane and banana. Unlike Azotobacter, it thrives in sugar-rich plant environments and can fix nitrogen directly into plant tissues, enhancing plant vigor.

Endophytic nitrogen fixation Fixes atmospheric nitrogen within plant tissues (up to 20–25 kg N/ha)

High compatibility with banana & sugarcane Boosts crop weight, tillering, and sugar content

Produces plant hormones Enhances root growth, early flowering, and cell elongation

Phosphate solubilization Improves phosphorus uptake

Improves photosynthesis Increases chlorophyll content, thus better photosynthesis

Reduces fertilizer use Reduces nitrogen fertilizer requirement by 25–30%

Soil Application Mix 2–4 L of liquid Acetobacter with 100–200 kg compost or FYM per acre

Drip Irrigation 2–5 L/acre diluted in 200 L chlorine-free water

Seedling/Set Treatment Soak banana suckers or sugarcane setts in 1:10 diluted solution for 15–30 minutes

Root Dipping Dip seedling roots in a 1:10 solution for 15–20 minutes before transplanting

Foliar Spray Not preferred—apply to soil for better colonization

Item Quantity

Acetobacter Starter Culture 1–2 L (liquid) or 1 kg (solid)

Jaggery or Molasses 2 kg

Rice or Gram Flour 500 g

Cow dung or compost slurry (optional) 2–3 kg

Chlorine-free water 200 L

Plastic Drum (Food-grade) 200 L capacity

Muslin Cloth or Lid For covering

Aeration pump or manual stirrer Optional, but improves multiplication

Make Nutrient Base :

Dissolve 2 kg jaggery in 5–10 L hot water, let it cool.

Add the solution to 200 L clean water in a plastic drum.

Add 500 g rice or gram flour (carbohydrate source).

Add 2–3 kg cow dung slurry for extra microbes (optional).

Inoculate Culture :

Add 1–2 L Acetobacter starter (liquid) to the drum.

Mix thoroughly.

Fermentation :

Keep the drum in a shaded, warm area (25–35°C ideal).

Stir daily or use an aeration pump.

Cover loosely with cloth to allow airflow and prevent contamination.

Fermentation Duration :

Allow to ferment for 5–7 days .

Observe froth, mild sour odor (good sign of bacterial activity).

Ready for Use :

Use immediately or store for max 5–7 days in shade.

Use Dosage

Soil Application Mix 20–25 L of multiplied culture with 100 kg compost/FYM

Drip Irrigation 15–20 L diluted in 200 L water

Set/Sucker Treatment Dip in 1:10 dilution for 15–30 minutes

Use only chlorine-free water .

Apply early morning or late afternoon.

Avoid chemical fungicides/pesticides 5–7 days before and after application.

Combine with phosphate solubilizing bacteria (PSB) for synergistic effect.

Best suited for banana, sugarcane, turmeric, ginger, napier grass , etc.
