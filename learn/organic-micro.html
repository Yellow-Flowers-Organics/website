Organic micronutrients

Organic-Micronutrients

The Organic On-Farm Micronutrient Solution is a natural, cost-effective way to provide essential trace elements and nutrients to crops using locally available resources. It helps improve soil fertility, promote plant health, and increase crop yield, all while reducing dependency on synthetic fertilizers.

This solution is particularly useful for correcting deficiencies in micronutrients like iron, copper, and other trace elements, which are essential for plant growth and development.

Promotes Plant Growth : Supplies necessary nutrients such as nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, iron, and copper.

Improves Soil Health : Enhances microbial activity, leading to better nutrient availability in the soil.

Cost-Effective : Utilizes locally sourced organic materials, reducing the need for expensive commercial products.

Eco-Friendly : Reduces reliance on chemical inputs, promoting more sustainable and regenerative farming practices.

200-liter water tank

20 liters of Waste Decomposer Solution

2 liters of Sugarcane Molasses

5 kg Mustard Cake

1 kg Moong Flour (green gram)

1 kg Urad Flour (black gram)

1 kg Besan Flour (chickpea flour)

20 Iron Nails (for iron content)

1 Copper Rod (for copper content)

1 kg Bajra Flour (pearl millet)

1 kg NPK (19:19:19)

Start by pouring 20 liters of Waste Decomposer Solution into the 200-liter water tank .

Add 2 liters of sugarcane molasses to serve as a carbon source for microbial growth.

Add 5 kg of mustard cake to provide organic nitrogen and micronutrients.

Incorporate 1 kg each of moong flour, urad flour, and besan flour, which are rich in proteins and amino acids that enhance microbial activity.

Add 1 kg of bajra flour as an additional source of carbohydrates.

Stir in 1 kg of NPK (19:19:19) to provide essential macronutrients like nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium.

Insert 20 iron nails and 1 copper rod into the solution. Over time, these will release iron and copper , important trace minerals for plant health.

Fill the tank with water (preferably non-chlorinated) to the top. Ensure all the ingredients are fully submerged and mixed.

Stir the mixture 3 times a day to maintain proper aeration and promote microbial activity.

Let the solution ferment for 7 days in a shaded area, away from direct sunlight.

Dilution : Mix 1 liter of the solution with 10 liters of water .

Application : Use a sprayer to apply the diluted solution directly onto plant leaves every 15-20 days . This provides direct nutrient uptake through the foliage.

Dilution : Use the same 1:10 ratio for soil application.

Application : Apply the diluted solution around the root zone to enrich the soil with nutrients and beneficial microorganisms.

Nutrient-Rich : Provides essential micronutrients like iron and copper , which are crucial for plant growth and disease resistance.

Increased Yield : Enhances flowering and fruiting, leading to better crop yields.

Improved Soil Microbiology : The solution fosters beneficial microbial activity, improving soil health over time.

Sustainable : Reduces the need for chemical fertilizers, promoting a more sustainable, organic farming system.

By using organic inputs like waste decomposer, molasses, and mustard cake, farmers can create a potent nutrient solution that is easy to prepare, environmentally friendly, and highly effective in boosting plant growth. This solution not only enhances plant health but also promotes long-term soil fertility.

For best results, apply the solution every 15-20 days either as a foliar spray or soil drench.

Yes, this Organic Micronutrient Solution can be used with chemical fertilizers. However, it's recommended to test on a small area first to ensure compatibility and avoid potential nutrient overload.

Look for yellowing of leaves, stunted growth, or poor flowering and fruiting, which may indicate a deficiency of iron, copper, or other trace elements.

The Waste Decomposer Solution provides a microbial base that accelerates the decomposition of organic materials, breaking them down into bioavailable nutrients that plants can easily absorb. It promotes the growth of beneficial microorganisms that help release the nutrients from mustard cake, flours, and NPK into the solution.

Molasses acts as a carbon source , providing energy for the beneficial microbes in the waste decomposer solution. It helps promote rapid microbial growth during fermentation, ensuring an active and nutrient-rich solution.

Mustard cake is rich in organic nitrogen , while pulse flours (moong, urad, and besan) provide proteins, amino acids , and other nutrients that stimulate microbial growth. These ingredients also release trace minerals that are essential for healthy plant growth.

Iron nails and the copper rod slowly release iron and copper into the solution over the fermentation period. These trace minerals are essential for processes like photosynthesis, enzyme function, and overall plant health, helping to correct deficiencies in the soil.

During fermentation, the beneficial microbes break down the organic materials in the mustard cake and flours, releasing nutrients such as nitrogen, amino acids, and micronutrients like iron and copper. The fermentation also ensures that these nutrients become bioavailable, making it easier for plants to absorb them when applied.

The solution should be stirred 3 times a day to ensure proper aeration. This helps keep the microbes active and ensures even distribution of nutrients throughout the solution. Without stirring, the mixture could become anaerobic, leading to foul smells and reduced microbial activity.

After fermentation, dilute the micronutrient solution at a 1:10 ratio (1 liter of solution in 10 liters of water) before applying it to crops. This dilution makes it safe for plants and ensures proper absorption without overloading the soil or foliage with nutrients.

This solution provides plants with essential micronutrients like iron and copper, along with macronutrients (from NPK and mustard cake). It promotes stronger growth, healthier leaves, better flowering, and increased resistance to diseases. The microbial activity also enhances soil fertility and nutrient cycling.
