Fish amino acid

Fish Amino Acid (FAA) is a fermented organic bio-stimulant made from fresh fish waste (or whole fish) and jaggery , used in natural farming (like KNF – Korean Natural Farming). It is rich in amino acids , enzymes, and micro-nutrients that promote vigorous vegetative growth in crops.

Feature Benefits

High in free amino acids & nitrogen Promotes lush green growth in leaves and stems

Contains enzymes, micronutrients Boosts plant metabolism and stress resistance

Stimulates microbial life in soil Supports soil fertility and root health

Easy to make on-farm Low cost, scalable, and eco-friendly

Suitable for most crops Fruits, vegetables, cereals, spices, flowers, and plantation crops

Application Method Dosage Frequency

Foliar Spray 3–5 mL per liter of water Every 7–15 days (veg stage)

Soil Drenching 2–4 liters per acre in irrigation water Monthly or biweekly

Compost booster 1 liter per ton of compost Once during mixing

📌 Best Time : Apply during vegetative growth stage (before flowering)

Material Quantity

Fresh fish waste or small fish 2 kg (min)

Jaggery (or molasses) 2 kg

Clean chlorine-free water 18 liters (approx.)

Airtight plastic drum 20–25 liter capacity

Wooden stick for stirring 1

Muslin cloth or lid To loosely cover

Chop fish or fish waste into small pieces.

In a clean drum, add fish and jaggery in a 1:1 ratio (2 kg fish + 2 kg jaggery).

Mix thoroughly. Add enough water (5–10 L) to submerge and ease fermentation.

Cover the container loosely with a muslin cloth or breathable lid.

Stir daily for 1–2 minutes to prevent anaerobic spoilage.

After 3–5 days, bubbles and a strong odor will indicate active fermentation.

After 15–20 days, the mixture will become brown and more liquid.

Strain the liquid using muslin cloth or sieve.

Store in a clean plastic/HDPE bottle.

Shelf life: Up to 6 months if stored in cool shade.

Application Type Dilution Rate

Foliar Spray 3–5 mL FAA per 1 L water

Soil Drenching 1–2 L FAA per 200 L water

Compost Boosting 100–200 mL per 100 kg compost

✅ Do’s ❌ Don’ts

Use non-chlorinated water Avoid chlorinated or soapy water

Ferment in shaded, cool area Don’t keep in direct sunlight

Stir daily during fermentation Don’t seal airtight – gases must escape

Strain before applying through nozzle Don’t use without dilution

Use gloves while handling raw fish Avoid inhaling strong vapors directly

Vegetables : Tomato, brinjal, okra, beans, leafy greens

Fruits : Citrus, banana, papaya, guava, mango

Flowers : Marigold, rose, jasmine

Cereals : Rice, maize, wheat (early stages)

Spices : Chilli, turmeric, ginger

Plantation Crops : Coconut, arecanut, sugarcane

Condition Shelf Life

Cool, shaded, airtight 4–6 months (liquid form)

Avoid exposure to sunlight Prevent degradation
