Silicon-Based Solution

Silicon (Si) is a beneficial element (though not classified as essential) that improves plant stress resistance , disease tolerance , structural strength , and nutrient efficiency . It is absorbed as monosilicic acid (H₄SiO₄) and deposited in plant tissues.

On-farm, silicon is commonly supplied through:

Potassium silicate (K₂SiO₃)

Sodium silicate (Na₂SiO₃)

Silicon solubilizing bacteria (SSB)

Silica-rich ash from rice husk, bamboo, etc.

Feature Benefit

Improves cell wall strength Enhances resistance to lodging, sucking pests, and fungal attacks

Enhances drought & salt tolerance Reduces transpiration and supports osmotic balance

Boosts nutrient uptake Aids absorption of phosphorus, potassium, and micronutrients

Prevents pest penetration Silicon layers under cuticle deter pest feeding

Organic input compatible Safe for use with Jeevamrut, Waste Decomposer, and natural pesticides

Strengthens cell walls Reduces insect bites, lodging, and fungal penetration

Enhances water use efficiency Improves drought tolerance

Boosts nutrient uptake Enhances phosphorus and micronutrient availability

Disease resistance Reduces powdery mildew, sheath blight, and other fungal attacks

Enhances photosynthesis More upright leaves, better light interception

Improves yield and quality Especially in rice, wheat, sugarcane, banana, cucurbits, tomatoes, etc.

Application Method Dosage/Instructions

Foliar Spray 3–5 mL potassium/sodium silicate per liter of water

Soil Application 2–5 L silicon solution per acre via drip or drench

Drip Irrigation Use diluted solution weekly in silicon-demanding crops like rice, banana, cucurbits

Seedling Treatment Dip seedlings in 1:10 diluted silicon solution for 10–15 minutes

Rice , sugarcane , banana , wheat

Vegetables : tomato, chili, cucurbits, cabbage, cauliflower

Fruits : citrus, pomegranate, papaya, guava

Flowers : marigold, rose, chrysanthemum

Item Quantity

Silica-rich Ash (rice husk, bamboo, or bagasse ash) 3–5 kg

Cow Urine (optional, improves extraction) 10–20 L

Jaggery (food source for microbes) 500 g

Silicon Solubilizing Bacteria (SSB starter) 1–2 L (liquid) or 250 g (solid)

Chlorine-Free Water 200 L

Plastic Drum (200 L) 1

Muslin Cloth or Lid For covering

Manual Stirrer or Aeration Optional

Dissolve 500 g jaggery in 5 L warm water and add to drum.

Add 3–5 kg of rice husk ash or bamboo ash .

Add 10–20 L cow urine (optional, boosts solubilization).

Fill up with chlorine-free water up to 200 L mark.

Add 1–2 L of SSB starter culture .

Keep the drum in shade, away from rain.

Stir twice daily to maintain aeration and activity.

Cover loosely with muslin cloth or breathable lid.

Final solution should have light brown color with earthy smell.

Use immediately or within 5 days , stored in shade.

Method Quantity

Foliar Spray 10–15 L of solution + 200 L water

Soil Drenching 15–20 L per acre (monthly)

Drip Fertigation 10 L per acre (weekly)

✅ Do's ❌ Don'ts

Use chlorine-free water Don’t store solution in direct sunlight

Apply in early morning/evening Avoid use with strong acid-based fertilizers

Combine with Trichoderma or Jeevamrut for better effect Don’t mix with chemical fungicides

Suitable for: banana, paddy, sugarcane, tomato, cucurbits, citrus, ginger, turmeric Not ideal for acid-sensiti
