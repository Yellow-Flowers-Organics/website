Fulvic acid

Fulvic Acid is a low molecular weight, water-soluble organic acid found naturally in decomposed organic matter (humus). It is a powerful natural chelator , helping in nutrient absorption , plant growth stimulation , and stress resistance .

It is lighter and more biologically active than humic acid and easily absorbed by plant roots and leaves.

Feature Benefits

Natural chelating agent Enhances nutrient uptake by roots and leaves

Increases nutrient efficiency Binds micronutrients like Fe, Zn, Mn for easy plant absorption

Improves soil health Enhances microbial population and soil enzymatic activity

Boosts plant metabolism Accelerates enzymatic reactions and photosynthesis

Anti-stress agent Helps plants tolerate drought, salinity, and pest attacks

Suitable for foliar and drip High compatibility for both application methods

Application Method Dosage Frequency

Foliar Spray 2–3 mL per liter of water Every 10–15 days

Soil/Drip Irrigation 1–2 liters/acre of diluted solution Monthly or at critical stages

Seed Treatment 5 mL per liter for 30 mins soaking Before sowing

📌 Ideal for use during vegetative growth , flowering , and fruit setting stages.

Material Quantity

Fulvic Acid Powder or Granules 1–2 kg

Jaggery (optional, for microbial boost) 1–2 kg

Chlorine-free Water 100 liters

Bio-decomposer (optional) 1 liter

Plastic drum (100–200 L capacity) 1

Stirring rod or submersible pump 1

Muslin cloth (for filtration) 1

Fill Drum : Pour ~80 liters of water into a plastic drum.

Dissolve Fulvic Acid : Add 1–2 kg fulvic acid powder and stir well.

(Optional) Add Jaggery : Mix in 1–2 kg jaggery to feed beneficial microbes.

(Optional) Add Bio-decomposer like OWDC: Add 1 liter to activate fermentation.

Top-up Water : Add water to make total 100 liters.

Stir : Stir twice a day for 1–2 minutes.

Ferment : Let the solution ferment in a shaded area for 24–48 hours.

Strain : Use muslin cloth to filter the solution before application.

Application Type Dilution Ratio

Foliar Spray 2–3 mL per liter of water

Drip Irrigation 1–2 liters per acre

Soil Drenching 1 liter in 200 liters of water

Seed Soaking 5 mL per liter of water

✅ Suitable for all major crops , including:

Vegetables : Tomato, okra, chili, brinjal, cabbage, leafy greens

Fruits : Citrus, banana, papaya, mango, guava

Flowers : Marigold, rose, tuberose

Cereals & Pulses : Rice, maize, wheat, soybean, moong

Spices : Turmeric, ginger, coriander

Plantations : Sugarcane, tea, coffee, coconut, arecanut

✅ Do’s ❌ Don’ts

Use clean, chlorine-free water Do not use dirty or chlorinated water

Stir daily during fermentation Do not apply without proper dilution

Filter before using through nozzles Do not mix with acidic chemical fertilizers

Store in cool shade in airtight bottles Avoid metallic containers or direct sunlight

Condition Duration

Cool, shaded area Up to 1–2 months

Airtight plastic bottle Prevents contamination
