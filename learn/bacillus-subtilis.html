Bacillus subtilis

Bacillus subtilis is a naturally occurring, beneficial Gram-positive bacterium that acts as a bio-fungicide and plant growth-promoting rhizobacteria (PGPR) . It is known for producing antimicrobial compounds that suppress soil-borne and foliar fungal pathogens, and for enhancing root and plant development.

Biological fungicide : Controls fungal pathogens like Fusarium , Pythium , Rhizoctonia , Alternaria , Sclerotium , etc.

Plant Growth Promoter : Secretes growth hormones like auxins and cytokinins.

Induces Systemic Resistance (ISR) in plants.

Produces enzymes that break down pathogen cell walls (chitinase, protease).

Enhances root colonization and nutrient uptake.

Improves rhizosphere microbial balance and suppresses harmful microbes.

Seed Treatment 10g or 10ml per kg of seed (mix with jaggery/sticker, shade dry before sowing).

Root Dipping 1–2 liters liquid or 2–4 kg powder in 30 liters water; soak seedlings for 30 minutes before transplanting.

Soil Application 2–5 kg or liters per acre mixed with 100 kg FYM/compost or 50 kg vermicompost; broadcast near root zone.

Drip Irrigation 1–2 liters per acre in 100–200 liters water.

Foliar Spray 5–10 ml per liter of water; apply during early morning or late evening; repeat every 10–15 days.

Bacillus subtilis (liquid culture 1 liter

Jaggery (or molasses) 1 kg

Gram flour (besan) or Soy flour 500 g

Chlorine-free water 200 liters

Optional: Cow dung slurry 1–2 liters

Clean plastic drum (with lid) 200 L capacity

Submersible pump or aerator For aeration

Muslin cloth or loose cover For covering

Prepare nutrient base : Dissolve 1 kg jaggery and 500 g besan/soy flour in 10–20 liters of warm water. Let it cool.

Fill drum : Pour this into the 200-liter drum and add chlorine-free water to fill.

Inoculate : Add 1 liter of Bacillus subtilis liquid culture to the mixture.

Aerate : Use a submersible pump or aquarium aerator for 4–6 hours daily to supply oxygen.

Fermentation : Keep the drum in a shaded area, loosely covered with cloth.

Stir daily : Stir manually twice a day if no aeration pump is available.

Ready to use in 5–7 days : Foaming and a pleasant fermented smell indicate it's ready.

Use within 7–10 days of preparation.

Do not expose to direct sunlight or rain.

Use clean, non-chlorinated water only.

Avoid mixing with chemical fungicides or copper-based products.

Can be multiplied multiple times using 10% of previous batch as a starter.

Recommended to refresh starter culture every 3–4 generations with fresh commercial culture.

Always test on a small patch before full-scale application.

Avoid applying under intense sunlight or heavy wind.

Use proper PPE if allergic to bacterial cultures.
