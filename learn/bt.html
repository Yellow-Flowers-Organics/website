Bacillus thuringiensis

Bt is a naturally occurring soil bacterium that produces crystal proteins (Cry toxins) toxic to certain insect larvae, especially Lepidoptera (caterpillars), Diptera (mosquito larvae), and Coleoptera (beetles). It is safe for humans, animals, and beneficial insects .

Species-specific toxicity Only harms specific pests, leaves beneficials unharmed

Safe for environment No toxic residue, biodegradable

Prevents resistance Can be rotated with other biocontrols

Organic certified input Approved in most organic farming standards

Crop Type Dosage per 15 L knapsack Application Notes

Vegetables, Cotton 5 g or 5 mL Spray during early larval stages

Fruit Trees 10–15 g or mL Target foliage where caterpillars feed

Rice & Maize 5 g or mL Spray at 10–15 day intervals

Item Quantity

Bt Commercial Starter Culture 100–200 mL or 20–50 g

Jaggery 200 g

Chickpea or Soybean Flour 200 g

Chlorine-free Water 10 L

Clean drum or bucket 10–15 L capacity

Muslin cloth For covering

Aeration pump/stirrer (optional) Optional

Prepare Nutrient Broth:

Dissolve 200 g jaggery in hot water and mix with 10 L clean water.

Add 200 g flour and stir well.

Inoculate Bt Culture:

Add 100–200 mL of Bt liquid or powder.

Ferment:

Keep the bucket in shade.

Stir twice daily or use an aeration pump.

Cover loosely with muslin cloth.

Ready in 3–5 Days :

Look for frothing and mild odor.

Use immediately or within 2–3 days.

Foliar Spray : Dilute 10–15 L multiplied Bt in 150–200 L water.

Use in early morning or evening for best effect.

Repeat every 7–10 days , especially in heavy pest infestation.
