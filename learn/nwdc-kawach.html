NWDC Kawach

NWDC Kawach is a specially formulated Natural Waste Decomposer Culture (NWDC) developed by DKC/NCOF for plant protection , soil disease control , and natural pest resistance . Unlike OWDC, which focuses on organic matter decomposition, Kawach is designed to control soil-borne pathogens and enhance the plant's resistance against bacterial, viral, and fungal diseases.

It is a probiotic microbial consortium that includes beneficial strains of Bacillus , Trichoderma , Streptomyces , Pseudomonas , and natural fungal antagonists.

Biocontrol of soil-borne diseases Controls Fusarium , Pythium , Phytophthora , Rhizoctonia , and nematodes

Improves root immunity Acts like a shield (“Kawach”) for plants

Reduces chemical pesticide dependency Can replace or reduce fungicide & pesticide use

Restores soil microbial health Increases beneficial microbes in rhizosphere

Can be used in all crops Works on vegetables, fruits, cereals, pulses, and flowers

Shelf life of 6 months (liquid) Stable for long storage under shade

Soil drenching Mix 2–4 L in 200 L water and apply to root zone

Seed treatment Mix 200 mL in 5 L water, coat seeds or dip cuttings before planting

Drip irrigation Apply 2–3 L per acre via fertigation

Nursery root dipping Dip seedlings in 2% solution before transplanting

Compost fortification Spray 2 L over 1 ton compost to make it disease-suppressive

NWDC Kawach Culture (DKC Liquid) 1 liter

Jaggery or sugarcane juice 2 kg jaggery or 4–5 L juice

Besan (chickpea flour, optional) 250–500 grams

Chlorine-free water 200 liters

Plastic drum (food grade, 200 L) 1

Wooden stick For stirring

Muslin cloth / gunny bag To loosely cover the drum

Dissolve 2 kg jaggery in 10 L warm water or use 5 L sugarcane juice.

Add 1 liter of Kawach culture to this mix.

(Optional) Add 250–500 g besan for microbial feed.

Fill clean, chlorine-free water up to 200 L in the drum.

Stir thoroughly and cover with a cloth (do not seal).

Stir twice a day (morning and evening).

Keep the drum in a cool, shaded location.

By Day 5 to 7, the solution will turn brown and develop a fermented smell.

Soil Drenching 20–25 L/acre via irrigation or drenching Every 15–30 days

Drip Irrigation 10–15 L/acre through venturi Every 2–3 weeks

Compost Fortification 2–3 L/ton compost During composting

Nursery/Seedling Dip 1:10 dilution, dip roots for 10–30 min Before transplanting

Foliar Spray (Preventive) 200 mL in 15 L water Every 15–20 days (optional)

Always use chlorine-free water Don’t use tap water containing chlorine

Store in shade and ambient temp Avoid direct sunlight or sealed storage

Use within 30 days after preparation Don't store for more than a month

Stir solution regularly Don't let the solution sit untouched

Filter before drip/fogger/foliar use Don’t apply directly if particles present
