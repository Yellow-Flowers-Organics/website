Jeevamrut

Jeevamrut is a fermented microbial bio-culture prepared using cow dung, cow urine, jaggery, pulse flour, and soil. It acts as a natural growth promoter, soil microbe activator, and mild pest deterrent.

Boosts Soil Microbial Activity : Enriches the soil with beneficial microbes (N-fixing, P-solubilizing bacteria).

Improves Plant Growth : Enhances nutrient uptake, stimulates root growth.

Low-Cost & Farm-Based : Made from easily available materials, especially from desi cows.

Organic & Eco-Friendly : Completely free from chemicals.

Works as Fertilizer + Tonic : Provides macronutrients and enzymes to crops.

Pest and Disease Resistance : Increases natural immunity of plants.

Dose: 200 liters/acre per application.

Frequency: Every 15–30 days during vegetative and flowering stages.

Apply with irrigation water, preferably using a venturi or through open channel after filtering.

Dilute 5–10 liters of Jeevamrut in 100 liters of water .

Spray during early morning or evening.

This is for 200 liters of standard Jeevamrut.

Ingredient Quantity

Fresh Cow Dung (desi breed preferred) 10 kg

Cow Urine (fresh) 10 liters

Jaggery (Gud) 2 kg

Gram flour (Besan) or pulse flour 2 kg

Local Soil (from under a healthy tree or bund) 1–2 kg

Clean Water 180–200 liters

Container (Plastic/Concrete Drum) 200–250 L capacity

Take 200L water in a clean plastic or cement tank.

Add 10 kg fresh cow dung and stir thoroughly.

Add 10 liters of cow urine and mix well.

Add 2 kg jaggery and 2 kg gram flour (pulse flour) .

Add 1–2 kg native healthy soil to introduce beneficial microbes.

Stir the solution clockwise and anti-clockwise for 10–15 minutes.

Cover with a jute sack or loose lid to allow air flow (do not seal).

Ferment for 3–5 days , stirring twice daily.

Cover the drum loosely and allow it to ferment for 5-7 days. Stir the mixture twice a day, morning and evening, to promote aerobic conditions.

Soil Drenching/Drip : Apply every 10–15 days during crop cycle.

Seed Treatment : Soak seeds in diluted Jeevamrut for 30 minutes before sowing.

Nursery/Transplant Drenching : Apply diluted solution before and after transplant.

Foliar Spray : Especially during vegetative and pre-flowering stage.

Always use fresh cow dung and urine from desi (native) cows for best results.

Stirring twice a day increases oxygen and microbial activity.

Prepare in shade and do not use metal containers (plastic or cement preferred).

Filter before using in drip or spray systems to avoid clogging.

Use within 7 days of preparation (best between 3–5 days).

Add Trichoderma or VAM for enhanced fungal/microbial activity if needed.
