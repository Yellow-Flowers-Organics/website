Rhizobium

Rhizobium is a beneficial nitrogen-fixing bacterium that forms root nodules in leguminous plants (like peas, beans, chickpeas, etc.). These nodules convert atmospheric nitrogen (N₂) into a usable form (NH₃) for the plant, reducing or eliminating the need for synthetic nitrogen fertilizers.

Feature Benefits

Symbiotic Nitrogen Fixation Converts atmospheric nitrogen into plant-available form

Increases Soil Fertility Improves nitrogen content and microbial diversity

Cost-effective Input Reduces external fertilizer costs

Environment-Friendly Sustainable and organic input, reduces nitrate leaching

Legume-Specific Strains High compatibility and efficiency with specific legumes

Application Mode Dosage/Instructions

Seed Treatment Mix 200 g Rhizobium powder per 10 kg seed using 30–50 ml jaggery solution as adhesive.

Shade dry for 30 min before sowing.

Soil Application Mix 5 kg Rhizobium with 100 kg compost or FYM and broadcast at sowing or near root zone.

Root Dip (for transplant) Dip legume seedling roots in 1–2% Rhizobium suspension for 30 min before transplanting.

Material Quantity

Rhizobium starter culture (liquid or powder) 1–2 L (liquid) or 250 g (powder)

Jaggery or molasses 1 kg

Chickpea flour / Gram flour (food for bacteria) 1 kg

Chlorine-free water 200 liters

Cow dung slurry (optional) 5–10 L

Plastic drum (200 L) 1

Muslin cloth or loose lid 1

Stirrer or air pump (optional) Optional

Prepare Nutrient Medium :

Dissolve 1 kg jaggery and 1 kg gram flour in 10 L warm water.

Pour into 200 L clean plastic drum with chlorine-free water.

Add 5–10 L cow dung slurry (optional for added microbial support).

Add Rhizobium Starter :

Add 1–2 L liquid Rhizobium culture (or 250 g powder dissolved in water).

Ferment :

Stir twice daily for 5–7 days or use an aquarium air pump for aeration.

Keep the drum in shaded, clean, and ventilated space.

Cover loosely with muslin cloth.

Observation :

Light froth and earthy smell = healthy culture.

pH should stay mildly acidic to neutral (6.0–7.5).

Application Mode Quantity / Method

Seed Treatment 200–250 mL of culture for 10 kg seed

Soil Application 10–15 L multiplied Rhizobium with 100 kg compost/FYM

Drip Irrigation 10–15 L per acre (filter the solution through cloth before use)

Foliar (less effective) Not recommended as primary mode; limited use

✅ Do's ❌ Don’ts

Use immediately or within 2–3 days Do not store in sunlight or in sealed tanks

Maintain shade and moderate temperature Don’t mix with chemical fertilizers or fungicides

Use crop-specific strains (e.g. Rhizobium leguminosarum for peas) Don’t apply in acidic soils (<5.5 pH)

Crop Suitable Rhizobium Species

Chickpea (Gram) Rhizobium ciceri

Pigeon Pea (Tur) Bradyrhizobium japonicum

Green Gram (Moong) Rhizobium sp.
