Inorganic micronutrients

Inorganic-Micronutrients

The Inorganic On-Farm Micronutrient Solution is a specially formulated solution designed to deliver essential micronutrients like zinc, copper, iron, and magnesium to plants through their root systems. By using a combination of Gadha Gomutra and OWDC (Organic Waste Decomposer) , this solution not only provides the necessary nutrients but also detoxifies chemical fertilizers, enhancing nutrient uptake and improving soil health.

Reduces Toxicity: Gadha Gomutra contains beneficial microorganisms that help neutralize the toxic effects of chemical fertilizers, making the soil healthier for plants and reducing the risk of harmful chemical buildup.

Boosts Nutrient Uptake: By breaking down nutrients into simpler forms, it allows plants to absorb them more efficiently, ensuring better growth and productivity.

Improves Microbial Activity: OWDC is rich in beneficial microbes that enhance the breakdown of nutrients and organic matter in the soil, ensuring that micronutrients are made available to plants faster.

Soil Detoxification: It helps neutralize the harmful residues of fertilizers and pesticides, restoring balance to the soil and promoting long-term fertility

200-liter drum

20-liter drum (4 units)

10-liter drum (2 units)

500 ml Gadha Gomutra

10 liters OWDC (Organic Waste Decomposer)

2 kg Copper Sulphate (Neela Thotha)

1 kg Seaweed Extract (80%+ purity)

1 kg Bio Fulvic Acid (80%+ purity)

1 kg Amino Acid Powder

5 kg Zinc Sulphate (21% Zinc, 10% Sulphur)

4 kg Ferrous Sulphate (19%)

7 kg Magnesium Sulphate

2 kg Manganese Sulphate

2 kg Urea

5 kg DAP

Rainwater or RO Water

In a 10-liter drum , add 1 kg Copper Sulphate (Neela Thotha) to 5 liters of warm water .

Stir thoroughly and let the solution dissolve for 1 day to ensure complete mixing.

Take a 200-liter drum and add:

10 liters of OWDC (Organic Waste Decomposer) ,

500 ml Gadha Gomutra , and

140 liters of water (preferably rainwater or RO water).

Stir the mixture well to ensure even distribution of ingredients.

Add 1 kg Seaweed Extract (80%+ purity) to the 200-liter drum.

Stir the solution until the seaweed extract is fully dissolved. Seaweed extract contains natural hormones that help improve plant growth and nutrient uptake.

Add 1 kg Bio Fulvic Acid (80%+ purity) into the solution.

Stir thoroughly to ensure it is well mixed. Fulvic acid enhances the plant's ability to absorb micronutrients from the solution.

Add 1 kg Amino Acid Powder into the drum.

Stir well until completely dissolved. Amino acids provide essential building blocks for proteins and help plants recover from stress.

In a 20-liter drum , dissolve 5 kg Zinc Sulphate (21% Zinc and 10% Sulphur) in 10 liters of water .

Stir until completely dissolved, then slowly add this solution into the 200-liter drum , stirring continuously to ensure even distribution.

In another 20-liter drum , dissolve 4 kg Ferrous Sulphate (19% Fe) in 10 liters of water .

Stir until dissolved, then slowly pour this solution into the 200-liter drum while stirring. Iron is critical for photosynthesis and overall plant health.

In a third 20-liter drum , dissolve 7 kg Magnesium Sulphate (Epsom Salt) in 10 liters of water .

Stir until dissolved and add slowly to the 200-liter drum while stirring. Magnesium is important for chlorophyll production and enzyme activation in plants .

In another 20-liter drum , dissolve 2 kg Manganese Sulphate in 10 liters of water .

Stir thoroughly and slowly add this solution to the 200-liter drum , stirring as you pour.

In the 20-liter drum , dissolve 2 kg Urea in 10 liters of water .

Stir well and pour the urea solution into the 200-liter drum, stirring continuously. Urea provides nitrogen, which promotes vegetative growth in plants.

In the 20-liter drum , dissolve 5 kg DAP in 10 liters of water .

Stir well and pour the DAP solution into the 200-liter drum, stirring continuously. Provides both nitrogen and phosphorus . Phosphorus is crucial for root development, flowering, and energy transfer in plants, promoting stronger root systems and healthier plant growth.

After 1 day , take the Copper Sulphate solution prepared in Step 1 and slowly pour it into the 200-liter drum while stirring continuously.

Copper is essential for plant metabolism and helps strengthen plant immune systems.

Once all the ingredients are added, stir the mixture well to ensure that all components are evenly distributed.

Storage : Keep the drum in a shaded area and cover it loosely to prevent debris from entering, but ensure air can flow. Stir the solution regularly (once or twice a day).

Mix 5 liters of the Inorganic On-Farm Micronutrient Solution with 500 liters of water.

Apply the mixture evenly over 1 acre of land during land preparation before planting.

This method ensures that essential micronutrients are applied directly to the root zone of the plants, allowing for maximum absorption and nutrient availability right from the start of the growing season.

Using OWDC and Gadha Gomutra not only detoxifies chemical inputs but also enhances microbial activity, leading to better soil structure and long-term fertility.

By ensuring nutrients are readily available, the solution supports faster and healthier plant growth, leading to higher yields.

Stimulates stronger root development and better nutrient uptake, leading to healthier, more resilient crops.

The use of locally available resources like Gadha Gomutra and OWDC makes this solution an affordable option for boosting crop productivity and Utilizes inexpensive, easily available ingredients to create a high-impact solution for crop nutrition.

Always use rainwater or RO water to ensure the solution is free from chlorine and other harmful chemicals.

This solution is not for foliar spray ; it is designed specifically for root applications.

Stir the solution regularly to keep the nutrients well-distributed and ensure maximum effectiveness.

The Inorganic On-Farm Micronutrient Solution is a specially formulated mix of essential micronutrients like zinc , copper , iron , and magnesium designed to boost plant health through root absorption. It is prepared using a combination of chemical compounds like zinc sulphate and copper sulphate, along with bio-enhancers such as Gadha Gomutra and OWDC (Organic Waste Decomposer) . These ingredients help detoxify chemical fertilizers, enhance microbial activity, and maximize nutrient uptake, improving crop growth and yield.

The Inorganic On-Farm Micronutrient Solution is important for your crops because it delivers critical micronutrients like zinc , iron , copper , and magnesium , which are essential for plant growth and development. These nutrients help:

Correct deficiencies that can affect plant health.

Boost nutrient uptake through roots.

Enhance resistance to diseases and stress.

Improve overall crop yield and quality.

Additionally, the use of Gadha Gomutra and OWDC in the solution helps detoxify chemical inputs, making nutrients more bioavailable and fostering healthier soil.

The Inorganic On-Farm Micronutrient Solution is applied during land preparation to ensure efficient nutrient uptake through the roots. Here’s how to apply it:

Mix 5 liters of the micronutrient solution with 500 liters of water.

Spray the diluted solution evenly over 1 acre of land during the land preparation phase.

This method allows the micronutrients to reach the root zone, providing essential elements like zinc, copper, and magnesium to plants for healthier growth.

The solution is absorbed by plant roots and supports early-stage plant development.

The recommended dosage of the Inorganic On-Farm Micronutrient Solution is 5 liters per 1 acre of land. To apply, dilute the 5 liters of solution with 500 liters of water and spray it evenly over the land during the preparation phase. This ensures the nutrients are absorbed efficiently by plant roots, supporting early growth and development.

Yes, you can use the Inorganic On-Farm Micronutrient Solution alongside chemical fertilizers or other farm inputs. The solution is designed to enhance nutrient uptake by reducing the toxicity of chemical fertilizers through the action of Gadha Gomutra and OWDC . These bio-enhancers help detoxify the chemicals, ensuring that nutrients are absorbed more efficiently by the plants. However, it’s recommended to test the solution on a small area first to ensure compatibility and avoid nutrient imbalances.

You need to use rainwater or RO water (reverse osmosis water) to prepare the Inorganic On-Farm Micronutrient Solution because these water sources are free from harmful chemicals like chlorine or hard water salts , which can interfere with the microbial activity and nutrient availability in the solution. Chlorine, in particular, can kill beneficial microbes like those in Gadha Gomutra and OWDC , reducing the effectiveness of the solution in promoting soil health and maximizing nutrient uptake by plants.

To store the Inorganic On-Farm Micronutrient Solution , follow these guidelines:

Shaded Area: Keep the solution in a cool, shaded place to avoid exposure to direct sunlight, which can affect nutrient stability and microbial activity.

Loosely Covered: Use a breathable cover, such as a cloth or mesh, to protect the solution from debris and pests while allowing airflow.

Avoid Airtight Sealing: Do not seal the drum airtight, as this can inhibit the beneficial microbial processes crucial for the solution’s effectiveness.

Proper storage ensures the solution remains effective for use.

Gadha Gomutra is an enriched cow urine bio-enhancer that contains beneficial microorganisms, nutrients, and enzymes. It is used in the Inorganic On-Farm Micronutrient Solution because it:

Promotes Microbial Activity : Gadha Gomutra enhances the growth of beneficial microbes, which help break down nutrients in the soil, making them more bioavailable to plants.

Detoxifies Chemicals : It helps neutralize harmful residues from chemical fertilizers, reducing toxicity and improving nutrient absorption by plants.

Improve nutrient absorption : It stimulates plant roots, helping plants absorb nutrients more effectively.

OWDC (Organic Waste Decomposer) works in the Inorganic On-Farm Micronutrient Solution by:

Boosting Microbial Activity : It introduces a rich microbial culture that helps break down organic and inorganic materials in the soil, releasing essential nutrients like zinc, copper, and iron.

Accelerating Nutrient Cycling : The microbes in OWDC convert nutrients into bioavailable forms more quickly, making them easier for plants to absorb.

Detoxifying the Soil : OWDC helps neutralize harmful residues from fertilizers and pesticides, improving soil health and long-term fertility.

Improving soil health : By promoting microbial diversity, OWDC enhances long-term soil fertility and structure.

Yes, Gadha Gomutra and OWDC can be used together in the Inorganic On-Farm Micronutrient Solution . They work synergistically:

Gadha Gomutra enhances microbial activity and helps detoxify chemical residues in the soil, improving nutrient availability.

OWDC provides a rich microbial base that breaks down nutrients quickly, making them more bioavailable to plants.
