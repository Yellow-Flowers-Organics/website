Humic acid

Humic Acid is a complex mixture of organic acids derived from decomposed plant and animal matter (mainly from leonardite, peat, or compost). It plays a key role in soil fertility, nutrient availability, root development , and plant health .

Feature Benefits

Chelating agent Helps bind and unlock essential nutrients in the soil

Enhances microbial activity Supports beneficial soil microorganisms

Improves soil structure Increases aeration, water retention, and reduces erosion

Stimulates root development Encourages fibrous and deep rooting in plants

Reduces fertilizer loss Minimizes leaching and improves nutrient uptake efficiency

Increases crop yield & quality Promotes healthy, vigorous plant growth

Application Method Dosage Timing/Frequency

Soil Application 3–5 kg/acre of solid humic acid OR 1–2 liters of liquid humic acid At transplanting, flowering, fruiting

Foliar Spray 3–5 mL/liter of water (liquid form) Every 15–20 days during growth

Fertigation 2–5 L/acre via drip irrigation Monthly or crop-specific

Note: You can multiply either humic acid powder or granules into a liquid form that is easy to apply via foliar spray or fertigation.

Material Quantity

Humic acid (powder/granules) 1–2 kg

Clean, chlorine-free water 100 liters

Jaggery or molasses (optional) 1–2 kg (to boost microbes)

Bio-decomposer (like OWDC/NWDC) 1 liter (optional)

Stirring rod or submersible pump 1

Plastic drum (100–200L capacity) 1

Muslin cloth (if needed for filtration) 1

Fill Drum : Pour 80–90 liters of clean water into the drum.

Dissolve Humic Acid : Add 1–2 kg of humic acid powder or granules and stir well until fully dissolved.

Add Jaggery (optional) : To promote microbial growth, add 1–2 kg of jaggery and mix well.

Add Bio-Decomposer (optional) : Add 1 liter OWDC/NWDC to enhance fermentation and microbial activity.

Top-Up Water : Add water to make up 100 liters total.

Cover : Keep the container loosely covered for 24–48 hours.

Stir Daily : Stir once or twice a day to aerate and ensure homogeneity.

Strain (if required) : Filter before foliar spray to avoid nozzle blockage.

Application Type Dilution Ratio

Foliar Spray 3–5 mL/L of water

Drip Irrigation 2–5 liters per acre

Soil Application 10–20 liters per acre via irrigation

✅ Do's ❌ Don’ts

Use non-chlorinated water Do not apply under harsh sunlight

Shake/stir before use Avoid using with chemical fungicides

Store in cool shade for up to 1 month Don’t overuse in high-salinity soils

Always filter before spraying Don’t store in metallic containers

Humic acid is suitable for almost all crops :

Cereals : Rice, wheat, maize

Vegetables : Tomato, brinjal, chili, okra, leafy greens

Fruits : Lemon, orange, guava, banana, papaya, mango

Flowers : Marigold, rose, jasmine, gladiolus

Spices : Turmeric, ginger, coriander, cumin

Plantation : Sugarcane, tea, coffee

Pulses : Moong, urad, arhar
