Panchagavya

✅ 1. Description:

Panchagavya is an organic growth promoter and bio-fertilizer made from five cow-derived products:

Cow dung (गोबर)

Cow urine (गौ मूत्र)

Milk (दूध)

Curd (दही)

Ghee (घी)

Traditionally used in Indian agriculture, Panchagavya is a potent microbial mixture that improves soil fertility, plant immunity, and overall crop productivity.

Feature Benefit

Organic & Natural Completely eco-friendly, safe for soil and water

Rich in Microbes Enhances soil microbial population and nutrient cycling

Plant Growth Promoter Promotes root development, flowering, and fruiting

Disease Resistance Boosts plant immunity against fungal and bacterial diseases

Pest Repellent Properties Natural deterrent to some common pests

Safe for Humans & Animals Non-toxic and harmless to handlers and livestock

Cost-effective Can be prepared on-farm with minimal input cost

Ingredient Quantity

Fresh Cow Dung 100 kg

Cow Urine 60 liters

Cow Milk (unboiled) 40 liters

Curd 40 liters

Cow Ghee 10 kg

Jaggery (गुड़) 20 kg

Ripe Bananas 240 numbers

Tender Coconut Water 60 liters (optional)

Water (clean, chlorine-free) 200 liters

Plastic Drum 200 liters

This will produce a fermentable and usable Panchagavya stock solution in 10-15 days.

Step-by-step:

Mix Cow Dung and Ghee:

In a plastic or earthen pot, mix cow dung with ghee and stir clockwise & anticlockwise for 3–5 minutes daily for 3 days.

Add Urine & Other Items:

On the 4th day, add cow urine, milk, curd, jaggery, mashed bananas, and tender coconut water. Stir twice daily.

Fermentation Period:

Keep the mixture in shade, loosely covered. Stir twice daily for 10–15 days .

Ready-to-use:

After fermentation, the liquid emits a fermented smell (not foul) and is ready for application.

A. Foliar Spray (on leaves)

Dilution Ratio: 3% to 5% (i.e., 3–5 liters per 100 liters of water)

Spray Interval: Every 10–15 days

Best Time: Morning or evening (not in harsh sun)

B. Soil Application

Dilution: 20 liters Panchagavya in 200 liters of water per acre

Apply before or with irrigation to boost soil microbial activity

C. Seed / Seedling Treatment

Soaking Seeds: Soak seeds for 20 minutes in 3% Panchagavya before sowing.

Seedlings: Dip roots of seedlings for 30 minutes before transplanting.

D. Drip Irrigation Application

Inject 3–5 liters per acre through the fertigation tank.

Crop Stage Panchagavya Use

Seed Treatment 3% soak for 20–30 min

Vegetative Stage 3% foliar spray every 10 days

Pre-Flowering Stage 5% foliar spray

Flowering & Fruiting 3% spray + soil drench

Post-harvest 5% soil drench to improve health

Always use clean, chlorine-free water.

Store in a shaded place, cover loosely to allow airflow.

Shelf-life: 1–2 months if stirred daily.

Always filter before spraying to avoid nozzle clogging.

Panchagavya should not be mixed with chemical fertilizers or pesticides.
